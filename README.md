## Research & data

### Traffic Delay Forecasting

For my MSc dissertation at **Brunel University London**, I built a Python machine-learning pipeline from more than **2 million traffic records**.

I compared multiple model families including XGBoost, Random Forest and neural-network approaches, applied cross-validation and a **250-fit RandomizedSearchCV**, and achieved a tuned mean **R² of 0.7474**.

`Python` `pandas` `NumPy` `scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `TensorFlow`

This work strengthened how I approach engineering problems: measure first, compare alternatives, understand failure modes, and optimise based on evidence rather than assumptions.
